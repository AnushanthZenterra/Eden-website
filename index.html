Common questions

When will Eden be ready to move in?

Eden is a presale condominium community in Willoughby, Langley with estimated completion between Summer and Fall 2028.

How much is the deposit to secure my home at Eden?

The deposit to secure your home at Eden is 10% of the purchase price.

Where can I view the Eden showhome?

Eden's presentation centre and showhome are located at 190 Willoughby Town Centre Drive, Langley, BC, open daily from 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM (closed Fridays).

Can I choose my own finishes at Eden?

Yes. Every Eden home lets you choose between two professionally designed colour schemes.

Where is Eden being built?

The Eden community is located at 19936 77 Avenue, Langley, BC, in the Willoughby neighbourhood across from the Langley Events Centre.

How many bedrooms does Eden offer?

Eden offers a wide range of floorplans including 1, 2 and 3 bedroom condominium homes.

Who is building Eden?

Eden is built by Zenterra Developments, a Lower Mainland builder with three decades of multi-family and single-family communities across the Fraser Valley. Zenterra designs, builds, markets and sells its communities in house.

Is Eden covered by a home warranty?

Yes. Like all new homes in British Columbia, Eden is covered by the province's mandatory 2-5-10 Home Warranty: two years on labour and materials, five years on the building envelope and ten years on structural defects.

Willoughby · Langley, BC

Location

Everyday essentials, green space and rapid transit, all within reach.

The neighbourhood

Rooted in Willoughby, one of Langley's fastest-growing communities.

Set directly across from the Langley Events Centre, Eden puts recreation, sport and community events at your doorstep. Willoughby pairs quiet, tree-lined streets with quick access to everyday essentials. Grocers, cafés, schools and parks are all within reach.

Highway 1 and the Carvolth Exchange keep you connected to Metro Vancouver, while the coming Surrey–Langley SkyTrain extension and Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) brings rapid transit closer than ever.

Transit is coming

Bus Rapid Transit is coming to 200th Street, with a station steps from home.

TransLink has confirmed the Langley–Haney Place Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) corridor as one of its first three priority projects in Metro Vancouver. Of the 13 planned stations, the one at 76th Avenue and 200th Street sits closest to Eden, just metres from your door.

BRT runs in dedicated centre lanes with signal priority, so it moves independently of traffic. TransLink projects it will cut corridor travel times by more than 40%, with buses every 10 minutes at peak.

South to Willowbrook, which is roughly a five-to-seven minute ride in dedicated lanes.

Buses stop every 10 minutes at peak frequency, with weather-protected stations and rapid boarding.

End to end from Langley to Maple Ridge via the Golden Ears Bridge.

Connected to the wider region

Willowbrook is the southern terminus of the BRT and the same station where the Surrey–Langley SkyTrain extension will stop. From there, the Expo Line runs west toward Surrey City Centre, Metrotown and downtown Vancouver. From Eden, you there are no transfers and no Park and Ride.

A corridor planned for the future

The Township's 200th Street 2040 Corridor Plan designates transit-oriented village hubs at 72nd, 80th and 86th Avenue. The 80th Avenue node, one stop north of Eden, is planned as "The Hub," anchored by the Langley Events Centre.

Station locations are confirmed and corridor design is advancing; full construction funding is still being secured. TransLink has stated the project can be in service within three years of being fully funded. Timelines are subject to change.
