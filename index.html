Solana, Willoughby, Langley · Condos · Completion 2027 · NOW SELLING

With spacious, modern interiors and 90,000+ sq ft of indoor and outdoor amenities seamlessly integrated with expansive greenery, this is a place where you can find the peace of mind that you crave.

It’s warmth and serenity.

Watching the morning rays dance over the trees, teasing the possibilities of the day. Basking in the soft glow as the world around you turns golden. It’s the excitement of finding new things to smile about every day. Solana is the peace that envelopes you in a sanctuary that renews your commitment to a gentler life.

A space to be mindful

A softer side of life awaits.

What does it mean to live a softer life? In our opinion, a good life isn’t about acquiring status symbols or leaning in, it’s more about laying back—prioritizing rest, the feel of grass under your feet, and the smell of fresh coffee being brewed while you’re still in bed. Be mindful. Embrace simplicity. Spend time in nature. Do these things and a softer, more enjoyable life awaits.
