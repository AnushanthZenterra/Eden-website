Eden: New Langley Presale Condos Now Under Construction

Construction is officially underway at Eden, Zenterra’s newest presale condo community in Willoughby, Langley. It’s an exciting milestone, and one we don’t take lightly given the current state of Metro Vancouver real estate. Thank you to the purchasers and realtors who continue to place their trust in us.

Zenterra remains committed to delivering quality homes. While many developers across the region are pausing or cancelling projects, we’re moving forward. With fewer projects breaking ground, the region is heading toward a housing supply shortage, making now a great time to buy a presale condo in Willoughby.

A great selection of homes is still available at Eden, and with shovels in the ground, purchasers can buy with added confidence, backed by real, visible progress.

Where Is Eden Located?

Eden is located at 19936 77 Avenue, centrally situated in the heart of Willoughby. Homeowners will be within walking distance of RE Mountain Secondary School, which offers an IB program, and the Langley Events Centre, with Highway 1 just three minutes away.

Connectivity here goes even further. Eden sits directly on the planned Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) corridor, with the future 76th Avenue and 200th Street station right at the building’s doorstep. When the Surrey Langley SkyTrain extension opens in 2029, residents will be roughly seven minutes from a connection at Willowbrook Station.

Few new condo communities in Metro Vancouver offer this kind of layered transit and lifestyle connectivity. Zenterra chose to build in this location on purpose.

What Is Included in Every Eden Home?

Zenterra has been named Multi Family Home Builder of the Year for 11 consecutive years, and at Eden that standard is reflected in every detail. Every home includes:

  • 9-foot ceilings
  • Air conditioning
  • Full-size stainless-steel appliances
  • A 5-burner gas cooktop
  • A gas BBQ connection on the balcony
  • Built in closet organizers
  • In-suite laundry
  • A storage locker
  • All homes with parking include EV rough in

The community also features over 90,000 square feet of outdoor amenity space, including pickleball and bocce courts, a fitness centre, a social lounge, and a co-working space.

Eden Condo Pricing and Deposit Structure

Homes at Eden are priced from just $374,990, with one, two, and three bedroom condos now available for sale. Purchasers can buy with a 10% deposit, making Eden one of the most accessible new condo developments in Willoughby, Langley.

How Can I Tour Eden?

Tour the Eden showhome at Zenterra’s Presentation Centre, open daily from 12:00 to 5:00 PM (closed Fridays), located at 190 Willoughby Town Centre Drive, Langley, BC. For more information, contact Eden’s Sales Manager, Jenny Chang (PREC), at 604-351-5151 or eden@zenterra.ca.
